A tsunami is a series of giant ocean waves caused by the sudden displacement of a large volume of water, usually by an underwater earthquake, volcanic eruption, or landslide.

Fast Facts & Awareness

Understanding tsunamis before they happen can save lives in Myanmar's coastal regions.

Did You Know?

Jet-Speed Waves

In deep ocean waters, tsunami waves can travel at speeds over 500 mph (800 km/h)—as fast as a commercial jet airliner.

Natural Warning

Receding Ocean Water

Before a tsunami arrives, the ocean shoreline often pulls back dramatically, exposing the sea floor. Never walk out to look—run inland immediately!

Myanmar Context

Low Frequency, High Risk

Tsunamis are rare along Myanmar’s coastline, which means communities are less prepared. Awareness and education are our best defenses.
