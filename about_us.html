Our story

Why We Created This Website

We are a group of students who wanted to explore tsunami hazards and understand how they could affect coastal communities in Myanmar. Through this project, we aim to make important information easier for people to learn and understand.

Our website brings together research about tsunami science, Myanmar's coastal areas, and practical safety preparation. We believe that learning about natural hazards can help people make informed decisions and feel more prepared.

  • Explain how tsunamis form and how they behave.
  • Explore coastal areas in Myanmar and possible risks.
  • Share useful preparation and safety information.

Our Mission

Our mission is to raise awareness about tsunamis by sharing clear, educational information and practical safety guidance for people living in or visiting coastal communities.

Our Vision

We hope to encourage a more informed and prepared community where people understand natural hazards and know the importance of taking safety precautions.
